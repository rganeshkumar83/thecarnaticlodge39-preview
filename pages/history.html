History & Sacred Lore of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39

Tracing over fourteen decades of fraternal labour, architectural heritage, and distinguished brethren since our foundation in 1883.

Home / History
A Venerable Legacy

Consecrated in the Dawn of Modern Madras

In the late nineteenth century, as Madras was evolving into an administrative, intellectual, and cultural capital of South India, Freemasonry provided a rare, transcendent sanctuary where people of different faiths, castes, and nationalities could sit side by side as brothers.

On October 27, 1883, a dedicated cohort of British and Indian brethren petitioned for the formation of the Lodge in Madras, formally receiving Warrant No. 2031 under the English Constitution on January 31, 1884. It was established with the noble intention of fostering harmonious camaraderie between distinguished local citizens and visiting brethren.

When the sovereign Grand Lodge of India (GLI) was inaugurated on November 24, 1961, the Lodge gladly opted into the national fold, receiving the proud and prestigious permanent roll number: The Carnatic Lodge No: 39.

Throughout its uninterrupted history of over 140 years, the Lodge has assembled faithfully, exemplifying the eternal virtues emblazoned upon its Consecrated Banner: Love and Truth.

Chronological Odyssey

Milestones Along the Masonic Way

From the 19th-century warrant of constitution to contemporary sesquicentennial preparations, these landmark events define our journey.

1883

Consecration as Lodge Carnatic No. 2031 E.C.

Constituted under warrant from the United Grand Lodge of England. The Lodge quickly established a reputation for ritual precision, intellectual lectures, and cross-cultural amity in Madras.

Foundational Charter
1923

Permanent Home at Freemasons' Hall, Egmore

Brethren of Carnatic Lodge joined other prominent city lodges in establishing Freemasons' Hall on Commander-in-Chief Road (now Ethiraj Salai), creating an enduring classical Masonic sanctuary.

Temple Foundation
1961

Affiliation with Grand Lodge of India

Upon the birth of sovereign Indian Freemasonry on November 24, 1961, Lodge Carnatic transitioned onto the national roll, receiving its revered number: The Carnatic Lodge No: 39.

National Integration
1963

Celebration of the 1,001st Regular Meeting

On April 20, 1963, brethren gathered in solemn rejoicing to mark the 1,001st continuous regular meeting of the Lodge—a remarkable testament to eight decades of uninterrupted labours.

1,000 Labours Landmark
1983

Grand Centenary Jubilee (1883–1983)

On December 8, 1983, the Lodge celebrated one hundred glorious years. The commemorative convocation was graced by the Most Worshipful Grand Master of India, M.W. Bro. Sitaram Jaipuria, accompanied by high dignitaries.

Centenary Milestone
2008

Quasquicentennial (125th Anniversary)

Commemorating 125 years of service, Carnatic Lodge expanded its Charitable Endowment Trust, instituting formal merit-cum-need scholarships for engineering and arts college students.

125th Jubilee
2018

1,500th Meeting & Banner Rededication

The Lodge celebrated its landmark 1,500th regular meeting. A solemn rededication ceremony was conducted by the Regional Grand Master, alongside the consecration of an updated lodge banner.

1,500th Historic Meeting
Present

Towards the Sesquicentennial Horizon

With 140+ years behind us, The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 advances towards its 150-year Sesquicentennial jubilee, digitizing historical minutes and extending charity across Tamil Nadu.

Ever Advancing
Fraternal Standard

The Consecrated Lodge Banner & Heritage

Dedicated at the Consecration of the Lodge on October 27, 1883, and preserved with reverent care across fourteen decades, our consecrated banner stands as the proud standard of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39.

The Consecrated Banner of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 • Motto: "Love and Truth"

Fourteen Decades of History

The Unbroken Standard of Our Craft

When The Carnatic Lodge was consecrated on October 27, 1883 in Madras, this magnificent banner was created to represent the dignity, unity, and noble aspirations and fraternal purpose of the founding Brethren.

Across more than 140 years of continuous Masonic labour, the banner has presided over thousands of meetings, welcoming generations of upright men who have dedicated their lives to moral integrity, brotherly love, and charitable relief.

📜

The Motto: "LOVE AND TRUTH"

Inscribed upon the lower golden scroll, these sacred watchwords remind every brother that sincere love of mankind and unwavering truth are the foundational pillars of our fraternal character.

🏛️

Historical Consecration (1883)

Crafted during the founding era under the English Constitution (Warrant No. 2031 EC) and proudly retained when the Lodge joined the sovereign Grand Lodge of India in 1961 as No: 39.

📍

Preserved at Freemasons' Hall, Egmore

Safely preserved within Freemasons' Hall on Ethiraj Salai, Egmore, the banner is reverently displayed during regular lodge labours on the 3rd Saturday of each month.

👑

Jubilee Rededications

Honoured during our Centenary Convocation in 1983 by the Most Worshipful Grand Master of India, and rededicated at our 1,500th regular meeting landmark.

Living Traditions

Memoirs & Anecdotes from the Brethren

The soul of Carnatic Lodge lives not merely in stone or regalia, but in the cherished memories and warm fellowship passed from brother to brother.

“

The Harmony of the Festive Board

"In the 1970s, after intensive ritual rehearsals in the Egmore temple, brethren of all ages would sit down together for our festive board. I remember eminent high court judges sitting alongside young college tutors, sharing laughter, toasts to the Grand Master, and stories of Madras of old. In The Carnatic Lodge No: 39, titles of the outer world disappeared—we were simply Brethren."

W. Bro. C. Ramanathan, PM

Initiated 1974 • 50+ Years in Craft Masonry

“

A Centenary Evening to Remember

"The December evening of our Centenary in 1983 was electric. When M.W. Bro. Sitaram Jaipuria entered the temple the entire hall resonated with dignified reverence. He praised The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 as an exemplary pillar of the Southern Region, commending our quiet charity and dedication to the genuine tenets of the Craft."

W. Bro. G. S. Balasubramanian, PGD

Past Master & Centenary Committee Member
