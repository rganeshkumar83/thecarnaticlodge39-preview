🏛️ Regular Meeting Notice

General Meeting Schedule

The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 convenes its regular labours on the 3rd Saturday of every month at 6:30 PM (with May & June recess) at Freemasons' Hall, Ethiraj Salai, Egmore, Chennai. Visiting Brethren in good standing from recognized constitutions are warmly welcomed.

Upcoming Labours & Convocations

NOV 14 2026
Regular Meeting Degree Work

November Regular Meeting & Third Degree Ceremony

🕒 6:30 PM IST 📍 Lodge Room No. 1, Freemasons' Hall, Egmore 👔 Dark Suit / Regalia

Regular monthly meeting and labours of the Lodge, followed by fraternal fellowship and customary Festive Board dinner. Brethren are requested to be seated by 6:15 PM.

DEC 12 2026
Annual Installation Grand Feast

Annual Installation of the Worshipful Master

🕒 5:45 PM IST 📍 Main Temple, Freemasons' Hall, Egmore 👔 Formal Evening Dress / Dark Suit with Collars

The grand annual convocation where the Worshipful Master-Elect is duly installed into office and the officers for the ensuing year are invested. Grand Lodge representatives in attendance.

JAN 10 2027
Community Service Educational Aid

Annual Higher Education Scholarship Conclave

🕒 10:30 AM IST 📍 Asoka Hall, Freemasons' Hall Campus 👔 Smart Casual / Brethren in Lounge Suits

Disbursement of tuition scholarships to 65+ deserving collegiate students from underprivileged backgrounds across Chennai and neighboring districts, in the presence of parents and faculty.

FEB 13 2027
Regular Meeting Masonic Lecture

February Regular Meeting & Symbolic Lecture

🕒 6:30 PM IST 📍 Lodge Room No. 1, Freemasons' Hall, Egmore 👔 Dark Suit / Regalia

Regular transaction of lodge business followed by an illustrated talk by a distinguished Past Master on "The Historical Evolution of Freemasonry in Southern India (1752–1961)".

MAR 21 2027
Social & Families Cultural Evening

Annual Masonic Family Gathering & Cultural Evening

🕒 6:00 PM IST 📍 Lawns, Freemasons' Hall, Egmore 👔 Traditional Festive / Smart Casual

A joyous evening of classical music, family bonding, and fine dining for the brethren, spouses, children, and widows of the Lodge.
