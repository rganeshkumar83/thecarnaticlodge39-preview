Charity Without Ostentation

Practicing Out of the Lodge What We Profess Within

In Freemasonry, charity is not viewed as a casual afterthought; it is an intrinsic duty and a sacred virtue that transforms moral tenets into tangible social progress.

For over a century, The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 has channeled the collective benevolence of its brethren into structured philanthropic initiatives that uplift the vulnerable, educate deserving youth, provide critical medical assistance, and rush relief to citizens during moments of natural crisis.

True to Masonic tradition, our charitable works are carried out without fanfare or commercial motive, guided solely by genuine brotherly compassion for our fellow human beings.

Charitable Initiatives

Our Core Community Programs

Focused investments that foster self-reliance, promote community health, and support families during periods of hardship.

Higher Education Scholarships

The Lodge’s flagship initiative. We provide merit-cum-need tuition fee assistance to underprivileged students enrolled in engineering, medicine, nursing, and basic sciences in government-aided colleges across Tamil Nadu.

  • ✓ Covers tuition fees and academic textbooks
  • ✓ Direct disbursal to recognized institutions
  • ✓ Mentorship by professional brethren

Free Medical & Optical Camps

Organized periodically in semi-rural and underserved urban areas around Chennai. In partnership with leading hospitals, we offer free doctor consultations, diagnostic screening, prescription eyeglasses, and subsidized cataract procedures.

  • ✓ General medical checkups & blood screenings
  • ✓ Free spectacles distribution to seniors
  • ✓ Voluntary blood donation drives with Red Cross

Disaster & Emergency Relief

Whenever Chennai or Tamil Nadu faces unforeseen natural calamities—such as severe monsoon flooding, cyclones, or public health crises—brethren rapidly mobilize essential dry rations, drinking water, medical kits, and clothing.

  • ✓ Rapid response food ration distribution
  • ✓ Temporary shelter rehabilitation kits
  • ✓ Collaboration with local civic authorities
