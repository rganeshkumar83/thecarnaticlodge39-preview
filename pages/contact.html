Fraternal Protocols

Information for Visiting Brethren

The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 welcomes Brother Masons in good standing belonging to recognized Grand Lodges worldwide.

📜

Credentials & Clearance

Visiting brethren must present their Grand Lodge Certificate or clearance certificate from their Mother Lodge, alongside a recent lodge summons or dues card.

👔

Dress Code & Regalia

Strict dress code: Dark lounge suit, white collar shirt, dark masonic or plain tie, black shoes, and the appropriate regalia of your rank/constitution.

🍽️

Festive Board RSVP

To join us for the traditional Festive Board dinner following the labours, kindly RSVP to the Secretary at least 48 hours prior to ensure proper seating.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Freemasonry a religion or a secret society?

No. Freemasonry is not a religion, nor is it a substitute for religion. It requires its members to profess belief in a Supreme Being, but does not dictate doctrine or theological creed. Freemasonry is not a secret society; its constitutions, meeting places, leaders, and charitable projects are entirely public.

How can someone become a member of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39?

The traditional Masonic principle is "2B1ASK1" (To be one, ask one). Read our step-by-step Membership Guide to understand the qualifications and petition process.

How does the Lodge fund its scholarships and medical camps?

Our philanthropic initiatives are funded strictly through voluntary fraternal contributions, member alms collections during regular meetings, and the returns of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 Educational & Benevolent Endowment Trust.
