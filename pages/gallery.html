Accessing Archival Records & Film

Historical video footage, centenary commemorative souvenir books, and archival lecture recordings are maintained under the custody of the Lodge Secretary and the Masonic Library at Freemasons' Hall, Egmore. Brethren in good standing and accredited Masonic researchers may request access for scholarly purposes.
