A Free Will Decision

The Principle of Personal Initiative

Unlike many civic clubs or commercial associations, Freemasonry does not solicit members or run aggressive recruitment campaigns.

It is a time-honored landmark of the Craft that every candidate must seek admission of his own free will and accord, uninfluenced by mercenary motives, business considerations, or unworthy persuasion.

Freemasons unite men of every country, sect, and opinion in genuine friendship, creating a moral sanctuary where political debates and religious controversies are strictly forbidden. If you seek personal enlightenment, meaningful brotherhood, and an outlet for sincere charity, you are encouraged to take the first step.

The Step-by-Step Pathway

How to Petition The Carnatic Lodge No: 39

A transparent and respectful journey ensuring that Freemasonry is the right choice for you and your family.

01

Initial Inquiry

If you know a member of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39, speak to him directly. If you do not know a member personally, you may submit an inquiry through our website to our Lodge Secretary introducing yourself.

02

Informal Dialogue

You will be invited for an informal coffee or dialogue at Freemasons' Hall, Egmore with senior brethren. This allows you to ask questions about the Craft and gives the brethren an opportunity to get to know you.

03

Formal Petition

Following successful interactions, a formal petition form is completed. You will require a Proposer and a Seconder from among the lodge brethren who can vouch for your integrity and character.

04

Enquiry Committee & Ballot

An appointed committee of past masters conducts a customary background visit. The petition is then formally read in open lodge, and the brethren vote by ballot in accordance with the ancient constitutions.

05

Solemn Initiation

Upon a favorable ballot, you are summoned to the Egmore Temple to receive your formal admission into the fraternity in accordance with the ancient and time-honored customs of Craft Freemasonry.

06

The Three Degrees

As you progress through your Masonic journey, you will advance through successive stages of fraternal instruction, deepening your understanding of Masonic virtues and joining a worldwide brotherhood dedicated to moral rectitude and benevolence.

Constitutional Criteria

Prerequisites for Candidates

Under the Book of Constitutions of the Grand Lodge of India, candidates for Freemasonry must fulfill several essential qualifications:

  • ⚖️
    Age Requirement: Minimum 21 years of age (or 18 years if the son of a Master Mason, known as a Lewis).
  • 🙏
    Belief in Supreme Being: Open to men of all faiths (Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Parsi, Sikh, Buddhist, Jewish, etc.). Freemasonry does not dictate creed.
  • 🎖️
    Good Moral Standing: A law-abiding citizen respected by peers, family, and employers with no criminal record.
  • 💼
    Financial Independence: Sufficient means to meet lodge dues and charitable contributions without burdening family commitments.
