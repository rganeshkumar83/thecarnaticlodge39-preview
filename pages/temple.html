Sacred Sanctuary & Architecture

Freemasons' Hall, Egmore | The Carnatic Lodge No: 39

A monumental colonial neoclassical heritage landmark on Ethiraj Salai, serving as the revered temple home of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 since 1923.

Home / About / Freemasons' Hall
A Centennial Monument

The Architectural Landmark of Southern Freemasonry

Consecrated as a permanent home for Masonic lodges in Madras in 1923, Freemasons' Hall on Ethiraj Salai (formerly Commander-in-Chief Road) in Egmore is one of Chennai's most distinguished architectural treasures.

Designed in the grand neoclassical Greco-Roman style, the building features a monumental pediment, fluted Corinthian columns, soaring ceilings, and exquisite terrazzo floors. For over a century, The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 has convened within these sacred walls, continuing an unbroken tradition of brotherly love, moral rectitude, and ceremonial precision.

Managed under the Regional Grand Lodge of Southern India, the premises house multiple consecrated lodge rooms, the celebrated Asoka Banqueting Hall, a historic research library, and lush surrounding gardens.

Inside the Sanctuary

Key Facilities & Architecture

Freemasons' Hall on Ethiraj Salai is widely celebrated for its dignified classical architecture, blending neoclassical proportions with stately assembly rooms and rich historical archives.

Classical Portico & Facade

Framed by imposing classical neoclassical columns on Ethiraj Salai, the entrance creates a commanding architectural presence that reflects the century-old heritage, permanence, and dignity of Freemasons' Hall.

Main Temple Assembly Hall

The primary meeting hall features stately proportions, high ceilings, polished woodwork, and comfortable seating, providing an inspiring environment for regular lodge convocations and fraternal assemblies.

The Master's Station & East Hall

Elevated in the East is the presiding station of the Worshipful Master, finished in polished teakwood with classical paneling and traditional fraternal furnishings where lodge proceedings are directed.

Asoka Banqueting Hall

The grand dining facility where brethren retire following regular labours for the traditional Festive Board. It provides an inspiring ambience for toasts, fraternal lectures, and bonding across generations of Masons.

Historic Archives & Library

Preserving handwritten minute registers dating back to 1883, commemorative centenary volumes, antique aprons, and rare philosophical treatises on Craft and Royal Arch Freemasonry in South India.

Charity Dispensary & Clinic

The campus houses diagnostic medical examination facilities where The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 and sister lodges conduct regular free health checkups, blood donation camps, and optical screenings for the public.

The Celestial Vault

Experience the Dignity of the Temple

Stepping inside the main temple room of Freemasons' Hall transports the observer into a realm of solemn peace and timeless fraternity. High vaulted ceilings mimic the celestial canopy of heaven, painted in deep blue and studded with radiant stars.

Here, away from the clamour of Chennai's streets, generations of judges, scholars, educators, doctors, and civic leaders have met together in fraternal fellowship, dedicated to the shared values of moral integrity, brotherhood, and community service.
