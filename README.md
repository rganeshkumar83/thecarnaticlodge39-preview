## 🏛️ Overview

A dignified, modern, responsive website for **The Carnatic Lodge No: 39**, engineered with clean semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. Featuring:
- **Mahadhi-style header** with interactive glassmorphic mega-menus, rich icon badges, and keyboard search (`Ctrl+K`).
- **5-Column footer** with signature giant watermark text **`THE CARNATIC 39`**.
- **Consecrated Lodge Banner showcase** displaying the official heraldic banner emblazoned with **"LOVE AND TRUTH"**, historical craftsmanship, motto "LOVE AND TRUTH", and unbroken preservation since 1883.
- **Dedicated Photo & Media Gallery** with category filter tabs and interactive full-screen Lightbox viewer.
- **Temple architectural tour** of Freemasons' Hall, Egmore (Ethiraj Salai).
- **Becoming a Freemason (2B1ASK1)** membership guide and 5-step petition process.

## 🎨 Design System & Mahadhi-Style Architecture

### 1. Navigation Menu (`mm-header`, `mm-nav`, `has-mega`, `mm-mega`):
- **Glassmorphic Sticky Header:** Subtle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`), dark navy backdrop, and gold accent border.
- **Interactive Mega Dropdowns:**
  - **About:** *About The Carnatic Lodge*, *Officers & Council*, *Freemasons' Hall Egmore*, and *Joining Freemasonry (2B1ASK1)* with a featured leadership card.
  - **History:** *Foundational Warrant (1883–1884)*, *Interactive Timeline*, *The Consecrated Banner Lore*, and *Memoirs of Brethren*.
  - **Labours:** *Meeting Calendar (3rd Saturday Monthly)*, *Annual Installation*, and *Festive Boards*.
  - **Charity:** *Higher Education Scholarships*, *Free Medical Camps*, and *Beneficiary Testimonials*.
  - **Gallery:** Direct link to the Visual Archives and Lightbox gallery.
  - **Contact:** *Secretariat*, *Visiting Brethren Protocols*, and *Location Map*.
- **Iconic Visual Badges (`mm-mega__ico`):** Every link in the dropdown features a custom icon, bold title, descriptive subtitle, and directional arrow (`→`).
- **Mobile Accordion Drawer:** Collapses into a sliding drawer on mobile (<1100px) with touch-friendly accordion toggles.

### 2. Footer (`mm-footer`, `mm-footer__big`, `mm-footer__bottom`):
- **5-Column Architectural Grid:**
  - **Brand & Affiliation:** Official crest, mission synopsis, and GLI / RGLSI badges.
  - **About & Heritage:** Quick links to history, leadership, and temple background.
  - **Labours & Media:** Calendar, installations, festive board, gallery, and banner lore.
  - **Philanthropy:** Scholarships, medical camps, and disaster relief.
  - **Contact & Venue:** Physical address at Freemasons' Hall Egmore, telephone, and email.
- **Big Watermark Text (`mm-footer__big`):** Signature `THE CARNATIC 39` typography styled with subtle opacity across the bottom of the footer, echoing the design language of `mahadhi.com`.
- **Bottom Bar:** Copyright, legal disclaimers, and smooth "Back to Top ↑" action.
