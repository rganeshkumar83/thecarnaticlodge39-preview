The Carnatic Lodge No: 39

— Motto: Love and Truth —

Welcome to the official fraternal portal of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39, meeting at the historic Freemasons' Hall, Egmore, Chennai. Operating under the sovereign jurisdiction of the Grand Lodge of Ancient, Free and Accepted Masons of India, we are dedicated to making good men better through moral rectitude, brotherly love, and charitable relief.

Fraternal Heritage

The Consecrated Banner of The Carnatic Lodge No: 39

Dedicated at our consecration on October 27, 1883, and preserved with reverence across fourteen decades, our consecrated banner stands as the proud fraternal standard of the Lodge.

THE CONSECRATED BANNER • MOTTO: "LOVE AND TRUTH"

A Living Standard

Fourteen Decades of Fellowship & Honour

When The Carnatic Lodge was consecrated in Madras on October 27, 1883, this venerable banner was solemnly unfurled to represent the identity, unity, and moral purpose of the Brethren.

Emblazoned upon its lower golden scroll is the eternal motto of our Lodge: "LOVE AND TRUTH". For over 140 years, these two guiding principles have inspired our members to practice unfeigned charity, cultivate genuine brotherly affection, and conduct their lives with moral uprightness.

🏛️
Preserved Heritage (1883–Present): Handcrafted in the late 19th century and carefully conserved through our Centenary in 1983 and beyond, the banner links today's Brethren directly with our founding members.
📜
The Sacred Motto — "Love and Truth": A timeless moral compass reminding every brother to practice benevolence out of the lodge as within it.
📍
Presence at Egmore Hall: Displayed with pride during regular lodge meetings and ceremonial convocations at Freemasons' Hall, Egmore, Chennai.

Historic Meeting Home

Freemasons' Hall, Egmore

Located on historic Ethiraj Salai (formerly Commander-in-Chief Road) in Egmore, Chennai, Freemasons' Hall is an architectural landmark and the historical home of Freemasonry in Southern India.

With its majestic classical columns, checkered mosaic pavements, and dignified lodge rooms, the hall has served as the meeting temple for The Carnatic Lodge No: 39 across decades of memorable fraternal gatherings, festive boards, and milestone celebrations.

  • ✦ Regular Labours: 3rd Saturday of each month at 6:30 PM (except May & June recess)
  • ✦ Festive Board: Traditional fellowship dinner at Asoka Hall following every meeting
  • ✦ Dress Code: Dark Lounge Suit / Dinner Jacket with Masonic Regalia

Heritage Spotlight

140+ Years of Unbroken Fraternal Continuity

From our consecration on October 27, 1883 under English Warrant No. 2031 to transitioning under the Grand Lodge of India in 1961 as Lodge No: 39, explore our rich history.

Oct 27, 1883

Consecration in Madras

Warranted as Lodge Carnatic No. 2031 E.C. during the colonial era, uniting diverse eminent local citizens and administrators in fellowship.

1961 - 1963

Grand Lodge of India No: 39

Became Lodge No: 39 upon the consecration of the sovereign Grand Lodge of India, and celebrated its historic 1,001st meeting in April 1963.

1983 - Present

Centenary & 1500th Milestone

Graced by Most Worshipful Grand Master Sitaram Jaipuria for its Centenary, and commemorated its 1,500th milestone meeting with banner rededication.
